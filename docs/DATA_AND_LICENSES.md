No pretrained text LLM, ASR, or TTS model is used anywhere, in training or at inference.

## Speaker split (simulator and Stage 1)
* AI voice: LibriSpeech speaker 3576. It is never used as a user.
* 31 training users / 8 held-out test users. The split is deterministic (`training/duplex_sim.split_speakers`, seed 0).
  Test users: 1673, 174, 1919, 2277, 2412, 3752, 3853, 8297.
* Stage 1 trains on the 31 training speakers plus the AI speaker, and evaluates on the 8 held-out speakers.

## Two-channel conversational data (not included)
Stages 2/3/5 on *recorded* dialogue need separate speaker channels and usage rights. Corpora such as Fisher and
Switchboard (LDC licence) or CANDOR (check its terms) would fit technically but were not downloaded. AMI headset
recordings (CC BY 4.0, multi-party) are a possible free option. `training/dialog_data.py` packs any such corpus
given a manifest of aligned channel pairs.
